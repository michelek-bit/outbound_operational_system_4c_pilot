### Rules that decide what counts

- **Qualified:** a second, different core service booked for the same customer, confirmed in Tech Assignment, with the customer’s stated need and approval.
- **Not counted:** cancelled bookings, duplicates of the same job, rebooks of the original service, bookings outside the service area.
- **Paid daily:** each CSR’s qualified cross-bookings from the day before, ₱100 each.
- **Quality first:** no pressure, no invented discounts; QA reviews a sample. Respect every “no”.
- **To confirm with leadership:** whether a booking cancelled after payout is deducted from the next day.
